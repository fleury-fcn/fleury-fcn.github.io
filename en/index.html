    Featured project · AI agent

    Halal Assistant: ask instead of search.

    An AI agent built into Halal Open Food Facts and Halal Open Beauty Facts. It queries the product database through RAG and answers in plain language, with a nuanced status and a link to the source record.

    • < 3 s

      response time

    • 85%

      accuracy on 200 test questions

    • 4.5M+

      products searchable in plain language

    What the agent does

    • Answers questions about a product.
    • Assesses halal compliance from the ingredients.
    • Helps users choose halal products that fit their request.
    • Helps complete a product record.
    • Guides users through the sites.

    Safeguards

    • Permanent warning: the AI can be wrong, check the label.
    • Nuanced status: compatible, ambiguous, label verified or only declared.
    • Every product cited links to its source record.

      • Nginx
